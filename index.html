Detailed Policy
🧬

Information We Collect

Short answer: nothing personal

⌄

The Interactive Human Body App is a fully offline educational tool. We do not collect any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, or any contact information
  • Location or GPS data of any kind
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or fingerprints
  • Usage patterns, session recordings, or behavioral analytics
  • Any information about the organs or content you view inside the app

The app functions entirely on your device without sending any data to external servers.

📵

Advertising

This app contains absolutely no ads

⌄

We are committed to an ad-free experience, now and always. Specifically:

  • No banner, interstitial, or video advertisements
  • No rewarded ads or sponsored content
  • No ad networks are integrated (e.g. AdMob, Meta Audience Network)
  • No in-app purchases that unlock "ad-free" mode — the app is always free of ads
🔗

Third-Party Services

None integrated in this app

⌄

We do not use any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, or external services. This means:

  • No Google Analytics, Firebase, or similar analytics platforms
  • No crash-reporting services that send data to external servers
  • No social media login or sharing integrations
  • No cloud sync — all content is stored locally on your device
  • No external CDNs or data calls at runtime

The only external interaction this app may have is through the App Store / Google Play for download and updates — governed by Apple's and Google's own privacy policies respectively.

👶

Children's Privacy

Safe for students of all ages

⌄

Because we collect no personal data at all, the Interactive Human Body App is inherently safe for children of all ages. We comply fully with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and similar international regulations.

  • No account creation or registration required
  • No chat, messaging, or social features
  • No data ever leaves the device
  • Suitable for classroom and home educational use
🛡️

Data Security

Your device, your data

⌄

Since the app does not transmit or store any personal data externally, there is no server-side risk of data breaches related to your personal information. All app content and any saved preferences remain solely on your device, protected by your operating system's built-in security features.

⚖️

Your Rights

Full control, always

⌄

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on our end. You have complete control:

  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored preferences
  • No account means no profile data held anywhere
  • No opt-in or opt-out needed — privacy is the default and only mode

If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us directly using the contact below.
